FAQ

What you may want to know
before contacting us

If your question is not covered, send us the specialty, date, and location. We will review and reply.

What stages can medical escort interpretation cover?

Support can include initial appointments, examination explanations, treatment or surgery consultations, pre-procedure communication, follow-ups, and aftercare guidance, depending on your needs.

Can you arrange same-day or urgent support?

Send the time, location, and specialty first. We will prioritize checking available interpreters, although same-day arrangements depend on current availability.

How is the fee calculated?

Quotes are based on duration, location, specialty complexity, multi-day scheduling, and any advance document preparation. Service begins only after confirmation.

How do you protect medical information?

Interpreters follow strict confidentiality principles. Personal and medical information is used only for service coordination and on-site communication.

Can clinics work with you long term?

Yes. We can build a long-term workflow by location, specialty, patient group, and time slot, or begin with a single assignment.

Do you translate medical documents?

Yes. We translate medical certificates, test results, consent forms, and aftercare guides between Chinese and Korean. Send the document type and page count to confirm the scope.

Will the interpreter give me medical advice?

No. Interpreters only convey information accurately. They do not give medical advice or decide for you or your doctor. Treatment and costs are decided by you and your doctor.

Do you ever shorten or soften what the doctor says?

No. We follow a no-exaggeration, no-omission principle and keep the professional limits of the doctor’s explanation.

I don’t know what to ask. What should I do?

Tell us the purpose of your visit and your concerns first. We will help you organize a list of questions to confirm with the doctor.

Do I have to book after asking?

No. You can send the specialty, date, and location to learn about your options, then decide whether to proceed.
